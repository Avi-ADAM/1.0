Integration proposal · November 2026

Give every solo creator the strength of a company.

Nas.com helps one person start a business. A connection to 1lev1 lets that person find partners and build together, from inside the Nas AI agent.

The gap

A one-person business is always a little fragile. One person cannot be the developer, the designer, the marketer and the salesperson, and cannot take a sick week.

Big companies win because they combine people. Solo creators rarely can: finding a partner, agreeing on terms and tracking who owns what is slow and awkward.

What 1lev1 adds

1lev1 is a platform for consensual partnerships. People team up around one product or one deal, and the work, resources and money each one brings become a transparent share of ownership.

Independents stay independent, and get mutual help and dynamic partnerships when they need them.

The agent searches, drafts and prepares. It never signs, votes or approves on anyone's behalf.

Three things a Nas agent could do on day one

Find a partner

"I need a video editor who'll work for a share."

The agent finds people by skill and open roles in existing partnerships. The other side's agent is matched to the same opening.

Form the partnership

"Set this up with an editor and a marketer."

The agent drafts roles, missions and what each side brings. The creator confirms with one click.

Track who owns what

"What's my stake? How was the first sale split?"

Approved hours and resources turn into ownership percentages, and every sale is split by them, visibly to all partners.

What Nas gains

  • A new capability for your agents that no single-user tool can offer: other people.
  • Stronger businesses on Nas. A creator with partners ships more and lasts longer.
  • Low integration cost. One MCP connection, no data migration, no product merge.

How it stays safe

  • The agent prepares, a person confirms. Nothing that binds someone happens without their own click.
  • No bosses and no vetoes. Partners approve, discuss or counter-offer.
  • Each user's own account and permissions. The agent acts only as the person who connected it.
